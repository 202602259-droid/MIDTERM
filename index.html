How results may differ

  • Official disclosures are best for registered franchise figures.
  • News articles may summarize or round numbers.
  • A total store count may include company-owned stores, while franchise count excludes them.
  • A page may show the year it was published, not the year the data describes.

CHECK THE EVIDENCE

Sources and verification

Use official records first. The comparison figures below are drawn from a 2025-reference-year industry data page; verify individual brand filings before final submission.

  1. Franchise-store counts, reference year 2025: KoreaCVS franchise data dashboard — lists MEGA MGC COFFEE 3,325; COMPOSE COFFEE 2,649; EDIYA COFFEE 2,562, labeled 기준연도 2025.
  2. Official disclosure comparison system: Korea Fair Trade Commission franchise information disclosure comparison — provides store counts and average-sales fields by year.
  3. 2025 franchise industry statistics announcement: KDI Economic Information and Education Center, 2025 franchise statistics — summary of the Fair Trade Commission release dated 2026-04-13.

Important: “2025 data” means the reference year for the counts, not necessarily the year a report was published. For academic work, open the official brand disclosure page and record the exact reference year and whether company-owned stores are excluded.

Conclusion

In this comparison, MEGA MGC COFFEE has the largest reported franchise network, followed by COMPOSE COFFEE and EDIYA COFFEE. This result shows relative network size, but more research is needed to compare profitability or business performance fairly.
